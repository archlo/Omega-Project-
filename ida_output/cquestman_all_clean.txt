CQuestMan::LoadQuestInfo(void) [0x6bec10]
  Loads QuestInfo.img + Check.img from Quest.wz.
  Parses series quests, quest names, auto-start/complete flags,
  time limits, exclusive groups, party quest icons, show effects.
  StringPool IDs: 0x1649=name, 0x1492=desc, 0xCC1=viewMedalItem.
  Also reads: autoComplete, dailyPlayTime, timeLimit2 from Check.img.

  Each demand includes: NPC, level range, items, mobs, skills,
  quests, jobs, morph, buff, date ranges, day-of-week mask,
  infoex, startScript, endScript, timeKeepInfo.
